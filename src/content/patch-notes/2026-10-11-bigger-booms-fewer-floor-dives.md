## 🆕 New
- **A brand-new PLAY menu.** Find Games now opens big mode cards: jump straight into Bowling or Survivors, head to Mission Control for Invasion, use a friend code, or join open games from all three modes. Fewer wrong turns, same number of pants.
- **Bowling Mayhem got a full makeover.** Mutant pins (Magnet, Mega and Balloon), lane events like Pinball Bumpers, Wind, Moon Gravity and a Conveyor, ball charges with PICK A BALL, and quick cards that explain each one the first time you meet it. The pins have formed a union. Negotiations continue.
- **Team chat is back in Invasion.** You can type to your own team during a match, and only your team can read it. Plot freely.
- **Bowling works with a controller pointer.** Press RB for a pointer that clicks whatever it's over, in every Bowling menu, even mid-throw for a quick trip to the Pro Shop.
- **Bowling quality-of-life.** Idle lanes now show a full rack, the host gets a START NOW button during the countdown, and tips stay up longer and can be closed ((B) closes the newest one).
- **Signs in Mission Control.** Wall panels and floor guides explain how to start an Invasion match and what it takes to play the aliens, without blocking a single pedestal.
- **Lobby send-offs.** Heading to Bowling, Survivors or Invasion now gets a proper farewell. We'll miss you. Briefly.

## 🔧 Changes
- **Explosions and gunfire are a much bigger spectacle.** Layered blasts, lingering smoke, thicker tracers, bigger muzzle flashes and gold headshot crowns, with the biggest upgrade on the new High graphics setting. Low keeps things light for older devices.
- **The aliens are easier to unlock.** The Collector License now needs account level 3 and two finished Defender matches, plus the briefing.
- **Minion Avalanche fights back.** The rolling boulder is smaller, weaves around, and flings minions at you. Bonus rounds now clearly say BONUS WON! or BONUS LOST, so you know who to blame.
- **Bowling dust toned way down.** Pin hits kick up much less dust, and near misses don't kick up any. Balloon pins now pop with confetti, because of course they do.
- **The lobby climb-grab is less grabby.** Mantling in the lobby only kicks in for real jumps at real ledges, and climbs are smoother. The obby climbs still work.
- **The lobby beta notice** now shows the Founder's Pack and the community-group perks, with a button for each.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **Survivors auto-shoot now starts on its own** instead of waiting for your first fire press.
- **Your first freeze ray or Med Gun beam after joining now stops when you let go.** It used to keep going on the first try.
- **The Giant's laser no longer hits walls twice per shot.** It was double-dipping. Rude.
- **Smoke looks like smoke again.** About twenty effects, including Commander rocket smoke and vehicle damage smoke, were drawing as faint glitter.
- **The weapon name no longer sits on top of the hotbar** on smaller screens.
- **No more falling through the lobby floor** after the obby's golden-pants spit.
- **Obby hazards stop resetting you when you're nowhere near them.** The lava was getting jealous.
- **The lobby beta notice no longer shows up squished,** and it now fits on landscape phones.
