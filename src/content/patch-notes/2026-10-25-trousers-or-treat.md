## 🆕 New
- **Halloween: seven free masks and hats to earn.** Finish Invasion matches, Survivors runs or full 10-frame Bowling games, in any mix, to unlock the Pants-Snatcher Witch Hat, then the Minion, Goblin Invader, Bone Collector, Commander, Overlord and Iron Mauler masks. Twelve finished games unlocks all seven. You can earn them through November 11, and they're yours to keep inside NOPAS. The Halloween card in the Lobby shows your progress. Each mask fits your head shape, which took us more tries than we'd like to admit.
- **Invasion skips Setup.** Matches now open mid-invasion: the Collectors arrive and the raids begin straight away. Nobody liked waiting around anyway, least of all the aliens.
- **Invasion: citizens need an escort.** They start scattered across Suburbia, follow nearby Defenders to a safe building at the Front, and move on when the Front does. Minions raid those buildings to carry them off. Lead a citizen to safety and you get the credit.
- **Invasion: drones and holding pens.** Two Evil Eye Drones snatch citizens off the street and tow them to the holding pens, where Defenders can break them out. The Commander and Overlord can fly a drone by hand from the command terminal. Shoot one down and it may drop its passengers.
- **Invasion: the Humvee is a moving spawn point.** It drives itself toward the Front, and Defenders can respawn at it. Hop in to take the wheel.
- **Invasion: pick your class before you go.** Choosing Invasion from PLAY or the Lobby welcome screen opens a briefing with every class lined up, so you know what you're signing up for.
- **Bowling: computer opponents.** Bowl against Officer Buckle and Dash on Easy, Normal or Hard. The lane menu now has PLAY SOLO, VS COMPUTER, WITH PLAYERS and PLAYERS + COMPUTERS, and beating every computer bowler earns a bonus. They play Mayhem too: they use special balls, join the bonus rounds, and lose their pants to a turkey like everyone else. Easy bowls like a beginner. Hard means it.
- **Bowling: the announcer talks.** Jerry B. calls every throw on your lane with more than 900 lines: strikes, splits, gutters, Mayhem moments, the pants gags and the end of the game, and he won't repeat a line until he's used the rest. He reads out the tips in your first game and nudges you when it's your turn. Turn him off with Settings > Bowling announcer (voice).
- **Survivors: an announcer, and a lot more talking.** Jerry B. calls the waves, bosses, Elites, supply drops, extraction and how the run ended, and tells you what to do when a boss changes phase. The classes have new lines for cards, curses and big moments, the Neighbourhood Watch has a few words, and the Understudy Overlord finally has lines of his own. Settings > Survivors announcer (voice) turns the announcer off.
- **Survivors: keep one power-up.** After a successful run, pick one of your cards on the Results screen and start your next run with it, already stacked up. It lasts until you leave the game.
- **Survivors: a guided first run.** Your first two runs are training runs. You go straight in as the Security Officer, a three-line hint walks you through moving, shooting and grabbing gold scrap, and a few weak Minions show up to practice on. Playing solo, wave 1 waits for you to finish each step. The timer literally says NO RUSH.
- **Survivors: free Emergency Trousers every run.** Everyone gets one free self-revive in each five-wave run. Training runs give you two, and they go off by themselves.
- **Play Solo.** Want Bowling or Survivors to yourself? Choose Play Solo in the Lobby's PLAY menu or welcome screen for a private game.
- **A touch screen for phones and tablets.** The Lobby welcome screen now gives Survivors and Bowling big one-tap tiles, with Invasion, the Arcade and the AFK Lounge right under them. No second menu in the way.

## 🔧 Changes
- **Anyone can play a Collector from their very first match.** The Commander and Overlord no longer need a level, two Defender matches or a briefing. Phones still play Defenders.
- **Minions don't bank pants any more.** They pick pants up and bring them to a Commander or Overlord, who does the actual stealing. Professional standards.
- **Pants a Defender is capturing stay theirs.** Aliens can't grab the pile out from under them; they have to stop the Defender first.
- **Defender bots head for the Front** instead of hanging around the holding pens.
- **The Security Officer's Button Bonanza knocks down a Collector carrying the Golden Pants.** They keep the pants, but they have to get back up first.
- **If you're out when a Giant shows up, your camera goes to watch it.**
- **Hits flash orange now instead of red,** and so does the Iron Mauler's slam.
- **The Lobby welcome screen got a makeover:** the NOPAS logo, character previews, a big Explore Lobby button, and an X to close it ((B) on a controller).
- **The Lobby follows the real time of day,** with day and night on US Eastern time, the same as the AFK Lounge. If it's dark, it's late. Go to bed.
- **Chat no longer announces every player who leaves.** The Lobby still tells you when someone heads off to a game.
- **The Bowling aim camera looks down the lane at the arrows and holds still,** with no more sway. You can still look around and zoom, and your bowler now stands where the ball actually leaves from.
- **The Bowling aim number is back,** from -1.00 on the left to +1.00 on the right. We took it out last patch. We missed it.
- **Mayhem hands out special balls at random:** one for every strike, every three spares and every lane event you win.
- **Bowling calls splits the way real bowling does,** and a Field Goal only counts when the ball goes cleanly between the pins.
- **Bowling's shortcut buttons (Emotes, Challenges, Boosters and Find Games) moved into the Settings menu** to keep the lane clear. The score card is back in the top-right corner.
- **Bowling has a Back button for mouse and touch, and Backspace on a keyboard,** and shows you when someone is about to join your lane.
- **Bowling on tablets and controllers:** aim controls are easier to read, controller hints fit on two lines, and Roblox's movement buttons hide while you throw.
- **Survivors is gentler at the start.** Enemies move slower in the early waves and have a little less health, squads take less damage, and the wave-5 boss is softer. Wave 1 sends a few more weak enemies, which mostly means more scrap.
- **Picking a card pauses a solo Survivors run.** Take as long as you like. Squads keep the timer.
- **Survivors squads stay fair when people come and go.** When a teammate leaves, the wave shrinks right away. A new player joining mid-run gets a free Emergency Trousers and some protection until their first full wave is over.
- **The Survivors squad bonus is bigger for every teammate.**
- **Phones and tablets auto-shoot the nearest enemy in any direction** in Survivors, behind you included. You steer, the gun aims.
- **New Survivors players get help with their build:** a suggested path through the early level-ups, a couple of free Orbital Sock Cyclone upgrades at the start of their first three runs, and a Results screen that points to a class they can afford next.
- **Emergency Trousers cost less in the Lint Shop and unlock sooner.**
- **Survivors Lint:** training runs always pay some, the first-run bonus pays however your first run ends, the Beefcake bonus fight pays more, and the Boast pays a little less.
- **Survivors extraction is quicker, with fewer meteors.**
- **Needle Eye's Flare shows its key on the HUD,** and says WAIT when you can't throw it yet.
- **The Survivors briefing opens on How to Play on phones,** and its text and controls are accurate now. It used to describe a slightly different game.
- **Survivors training runs have a calmer HUD,** and their final fight can't fail you on the timer.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **Pants no longer vanish when the minion carrying them is defeated.**
- **Quick Connect pipes in the holding pen follow your cursor,** so jailbreaks work at full speed. We were reading your mouse half a square too low.
- **Survivors power-up cards no longer cover the pause menu or the downed screen.**
- **Survivors AUTO hits enemies right next to you more reliably.**
- **A Golem Cycle golem isn't treated as the boss any more,** so there's no BOSS DOWN banner when one falls mid-wave.
- **Survivors' PLAY, SHOP and CLASS PATHS buttons stay hidden during a run,** and captions on phones sit clear of the touch buttons.
- **Survivors on phones:** purchase confirmations and the Results screen no longer get cut off or overlap, and next-run goals fit without scrolling.
- **Scrap and swarm sounds pause when the run does.**
- **The revive screen only says NO TROUSERS LEFT if you had some to begin with.**
