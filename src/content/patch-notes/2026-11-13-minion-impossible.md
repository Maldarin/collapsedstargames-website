## 🆕 New
- **Steal a Minion is open to everyone.** Find it in the Lobby with a NEW sticker. Buy minions off the belt and they earn Bounty at your base. Then sneak onto other players' lots, nab their minions and carry them home. They can do the same to you, so keep an eye on your pods.
- **More ways to get minions.** Sneak through the Evidence Locker while Dozy naps, get past the guards in Guarded Salvage, or take a job at the Dirty Laundry desk.
- **Three Dirty Laundry jobs: Puff Round-Up, Probably Pants and Power Nap.** Walk into things to pick them up, stack them on your head and carry them to where they belong. No buttons needed.
- **Arena battles.** Build a team of minions and battle in free Practice or the League. Your Arena Team holds up to six minions that can't be nabbed from your base.
- **Minion fights look like fights.** Minions throw punches, kicks, drop-kicks, flying punches and head bonks, with sounds to match, and they react when they're hit. On phones, the camera moves in close for the action. The chest bump is still allowed, just not every time.
- **Challenge someone on your server** to a friendly Arena match. Nothing is at stake, so it's purely for bragging rights.
- **The Minion Index** records every kind of minion you've ever had, Golden and Rainbow ones included, even after they've left your base. Filling it in earns 16 trophies.
- **Golden and Rainbow minions can turn up in the Locker,** so even the rarest kinds can be shiny. A Rainbow Overlord-in-Training finally looks the part.
- **First Steps shows new players the ropes,** including a practice nab from Deputy Doodle, a robot who is very good at being robbed. Nobody can nab from new players while they're still learning.
- **If someone nabs your minion, you'll know.** A card tells you who to chase. If they get it home, it points you at their lot so you can NAB IT BACK.
- **Pants Rush: WANT A CREW?** After the tutorial, you can keep your run to yourself, open it to friends, or open it to everyone. Other players can join while you're mid-run.
- **New to Pants Rush? One tap starts your first raid,** tutorial included. Everything else is under "More options".
- **Pants Rush has a pants meter.** It fills as you bank pants, with a "+N!" pop and a bell each time. If time runs out, a card tells you how many pants short you were.
- **Depantsed Pants Rush citizens shout about their shorts.** Everyone's shorts deserve to be seen.
- **The Pants Rush victory card names your Pants Puller,** and the next boss appears on a WANTED poster.
- **Pants Rush medals and Gold titles.** Your best medal for each phase shows on the phase picker, and Gold medals earn free titles.
- **Fancy Pants VIP.** A very well-dressed citizen strolls through each Pants Rush phase. Catch them for bonus pants.
- **RAMPAGE HAUL.** In the Pants Factory finale, giant stomps now depants people instead of knocking them out, and a counter keeps score.
- **A better first minute in Survivors.** On a new player's first run, the Minions come to you even if you stand still, a big one drops your first card, a starter chest pops open, and then a crowd arrives.
- **Thumb Toss.** On phones and tablets, tap the Starch Bomb or Virus Grenade slot to throw it at the biggest crowd. You go straight back to your gun afterwards.
- **Try-On Rack.** Each locked Survivors class gets one free run, so you can try it before you unlock it.
- **Say Pants!** At Survivors Results, each squad member can earn a sash for what they did most. The new Squad Photo button poses everyone for a picture you can save.
- **Caught Short.** Once a wave, getting knocked down in Survivors lets a Minion run off with your pants. Shoot it before it gets away to get back up. It never happens in training runs.
- **The Survivors kill counter is back** on the wave line, with a gold pop and a bell at big milestones.

## 🔧 Changes
- **Arena battles play at normal speed,** so you can actually see the hits. The turn clock is big and changes color as time runs low, and orders you've queued go in even if you don't lock in.
- **Arena minions hit harder,** so battles end sooner.
- **Step away from Steal a Minion for a while and you go Off Duty on your own.** One tap clocks you back in. A lot that just nabbed from another player can't hide for a few minutes. Fair's fair.
- **Steal a Minion keeps trying if your save is slow to load,** and says "Your save is loading…" instead of starting you on an empty base.
- **Pants Rush bosses on Easy get two health bars and some gentle tricks** too.
- **The Pants Rush factory finale opens with defenders already on guard.** It was a bit quiet in there.
- **Halloween is over.** Its button and billboards have left the Lobby. Anything you earned is yours to keep.
- **More Survivors enemies when your squad is small,** especially in the Pants Factory and on the Mothership.
- **Survivors waves don't go empty any more.** If your squad clears a wave's enemies early, weaker ones keep coming until near the end.
- **New Survivors players get more help.** Their first two runs have a wider scrap pickup. If they go a while without a card, nearby scrap flies to them. The training finale is softer too.
- **Survivors training runs move faster:** shorter breaks, no Curse cards, no boon vote and no Bonus round.
- **Your first full Survivors run keeps the training wheels.** Emergency Trousers still go off by themselves, and auto-shoot still aims in every direction.
- **On phones and tablets, Survivors auto-shoot targets enemies in front of you** (and anything very close). Turn to face the crowd.
- **Leave a solo Survivors run at a break and you keep everything you earned.**
- **The mini-boss chest is worth watching.** Playing solo, the world stops while it opens. In a squad, it pushes enemies back. A bigger haul gets a bigger show, and the loot shows up just above your head.
- **Survivors SWAP cards say what the power you're giving up does,** and the Keep One Power-up chips say what each card does.
- **The Survivors Results card leads with what matters:** what you can buy, your new bests and your next goal.
- **Picking Survivors cards in a solo run is quicker.** On phones the cards open by themselves, and on desktop a click reminds you to press 1, 2 or 3.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **The Pants Rush tutorial no longer loops back to ARM WEAPON 1** instead of teaching you to depants.
- **Panicking citizens no longer block or soak up a depants beam,** in Pants Rush and in Invasion.
- **Mouse players can see their pointer on the Pants Rush tutorial and crew cards,** and the weapon keys still work while those cards are up.
- **Skip Tutorial works on the Pants Rush aiming card** with Backspace or (B).
- **Pants Rush boss names and titles no longer overlap.**
- **The Founder's Emergency Pants Supply backpack spills pants in every game now.**
- **Survivors bosses no longer get stuck behind the Pants Factory pillars.** The Laundro-Golem had found a nice spot and wasn't planning to leave.
- **On a controller, you can close the Survivors power-ups panel when level-up cards appear.** Before, you could get stuck.
