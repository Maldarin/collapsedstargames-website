## 🆕 New
- **New touch buttons in Invasion and Survivors.** Phones and tablets get round see-through buttons with icons, grouped around JUMP. A ring fills back up while a move recharges. There are fewer buttons to hit, too: push the stick all the way to sprint, guns top themselves up when you stop firing, the Needle Eye goes prone when he zooms in and stands still, and the Athlete tosses minions by himself when two are in reach. Your thumbs have been promoted.
- **Invasion on touch: one tap does it.** Tap Capture once to start capturing pants, then move and shoot while it counts (on a controller, one press does the same). The Engineer builds a turret or dispenser with two taps and keeps his gun out while he does it. The Needle Eye's grapple snaps to a rooftop edge when you aim close to one. A Citizen with a strong charm emote gets a CHARM button.
- **The Overlord works on tablets.** POSSESS and AIRDROP sit on his weapon bar, possessing a minion works on touch, and a big gold GIVE PANTS! button appears when he can hand over the Golden Pants.
- **Invasion shows you where to go.** A gold marker points at the Front, or at the citizen or shelter you're escorting, with the distance. When it's off screen, an arrow at the edge of your screen points the way.
- **Invasion teaches your class's first job.** Your first time on a class, the card shows your basic job, and on touch a yellow ring and arrow point at the button you need. Do it once and you get a "✓ NICE!". New Commanders and Overlords get three short lines on how to steal and bank pants.
- **Short tips for big firsts:** your first loose pile of pants, your first grab as a Collector, and the first time you get caught. Each one shows once.
- **Phones get the objective line back.** It shows for a few seconds at the top of the screen, then gets out of the way. Tap the new "?" button any time to see it again.
- **The Invasion class page has a WATCH: HOW TO PLAY button,** plus difficulty stars on every class. The Security Officer is marked BEST FIRST PICK and is already selected, so new players can hit PLAY straight away.
- **A clearer knockout card.** It says who got you ("DEPANTSED BY BOB!"), gives one short tip for your class and counts down until you're back. All the tips are true now. Some of the old ones weren't.
- **The match results say why you won or lost,** plus one thing you did well.
- **The Invasion pants counter is easier to read:** "ALIENS HAVE 12 / 34", with the bar filling toward the mothership. A card warns you when the aliens are halfway there.
- **Caught Citizens who press HELP now see "Help is coming!"** when the team really has been told, or "Help already called!" if they press again too soon. Before, it just showed your own name back at you.
- **Players who join an Invasion match late get one card** that tells them where the fight is, instead of the whole beginner speech.
- **Bowling lane lightshows.** Good hits, spares and strikes set off colored runway lights, laser fans and sparkles on your lane, and streaks go full rainbow.
- **Real Bowling personal bests.** Your score card shows your best ever score for each mode, and NEW BEST! only appears when you actually beat it.
- **PLAY AGAIN in Bowling.** Bowling alone (or with computer opponents only)? One button starts your next game.
- **Bowling shows a live pin count while the pins are still falling,** then swaps it for the official count.
- **Four new Bowling titles to unlock early on.** Results shows your next unlock and how much daily bonus you have left.
- **Survivors PLAY AGAIN lets you pick the next map.** Locked maps show how to unlock them. If nobody picks, you replay the map you just played.

## 🔧 Changes
- **Survivors waves are shorter, especially the later ones.** The pace is the same, and scrap is worth more, so you level up just as often. Beat the wave-5 boss and the clock skips almost to the end.
- **Survivors on Cul-de-sac is a little easier on a normal run,** and every class gets its health back a little faster in Survivors.
- **The Survivors Boast bonus round is a little harder.**
- **Survivors training runs aim for you on PC too.** After training, the HUD reminds you that AUTO goes back to aiming with your camera.
- **Lots of Survivors cards now say what they actually do.** Cards that would do nothing for you (dash cards after Static Cling, for one) aren't offered any more. Phone and tablet cards show their rarity colors like desktop ones.
- **The Survivors HUD reads better.** The wave line shows how far into the run you are ("WAVE 3/5"), banners pop in and fade out, a loss on Results tells you why, and the briefing shows the difficulty vote and whether your daily Lint bonus is ready.
- **Survivors tells you before you join a run you can't play yet** (or one that pays joiners nothing, like Endless).
- **When a Survivors teammate saves themselves with Emergency Trousers, the squad sees it.** Teammates also show by display name now.
- **Killing a Closet Blinker drops the scrap it stole,** and anyone can pick it up. It also steals only on bites that actually land.
- **Survivors with a controller:** hold (A) to ready up, the D-pad picks cards by direction, and (B) closes the wave recap.
- **Invasion messages take turns** instead of piling on top of each other, and the same news is only announced once.
- **Invasion cards now name the Front:** "HOLD LAUNDROMAT 24!"
- **Players who've seen the beginner cards don't get them every match any more.** Each one shows once per account. It was supposed to work that way all along, but the game kept forgetting it had told you.
- **The match result banners are plainer:** "DEFENDERS WIN — PANTS SAFE!" and "ALIENS WIN — GOLDEN PANTS TAKEN!"
- **Bot speech bubbles are off by default.** They were blocking the view, and the bots weren't saying anything important.
- **On phones and tablets, aim assist pulls harder toward minions and bots.** Against other players it's the same as before.
- **Touch players get their stamina back a little faster in Invasion.**
- **New players who leave their first few Invasion matches early aren't penalized for it.**
- **Bowling moves faster between throws.** The pins reset quicker and the gags wrap up sooner.
- **Bowling is kinder to beginners.** Slowpoke jokes only go to the slow player, and only when someone is waiting on them. The Turkey Trot leaves first-game bowlers alone. Lost pants come back by themselves after a couple of throws. And it's "PANTS-PAL" now.
- **Bowling on phones:** the throw meters sit in the middle of the screen, the full scorecard fits, the lane lobby has two columns, and banners, cards and Arcade buttons stay out of the way while you throw.
- **The Bowling lane lobby has a big BOWL! button,** and if you can't join a lane, it tells you why.
- **Roblox's Reduce Motion setting now works in NOPAS:** less shake, flashing and bouncing in every mode, including Bowling's storm lightning.
- **PlayStation controllers show PlayStation button names.** Consoles show controller hints from the moment you join.
- **Text is bigger on TVs,** and small button text has a minimum size everywhere.
- **Phones and tablets get fitted screens across the game:** the Lobby landing, Quartermaster, Hall of Pants, Settings, Survivors results and bonus votes, and the Lobby's side activities. Tablets show the Invasion event feed, and the AFK Lounge has an EMOTES button on touch.
- **Bowling and the AFK Lounge stay sideways on phones and tablets.**
- **Collapsed Star Games group members get double the XP bonus.**

## 🐛 Fixes
- **Survivors boss attacks show ground warnings at their real size,** and leap landings show where they'll actually hit.
- **Golem Cycle works the right way round.** The golems were taking reduced damage and hitting at full power, when it was meant to be the opposite. Those waves are much easier now.
- **Rift Shift opens its rift inside the barrier,** where you can actually close it.
- **Cul-de-sac's ranged enemies stay inside the fence,** where your shots can reach them.
- **Survivors enemies glide instead of hopping around.**
- **Survivors players can't get stuck any more** after a failed trip back to the lobby, a bonus round that wouldn't end, or a slow teammate during the arena swap.
- **A solo Survivors player who wins the Boast no longer freezes on the win screen.**
- **Survivors bosses don't skip a phase when you stagger them** or they're mid-jump.
- **Tripwire Net does the damage its card promises,** and the Styx river lasts as long as it says.
- **Survivors pop-up turrets have their bodies back.** They'd been floating high above the map.
- **A Lint Shop left open before a Survivors run closes when wave 1 starts,** so you can move and shoot from the first second.
- **Big Survivors squads can see every minion on wave 1,** and scrap no longer turns invisible or leaves ghost orbs when the ground gets crowded.
- **Neighbourhood Watch citizens don't get stuck or let go halfway through a revive.**
- **Holding (B) to reroll Survivors cards no longer makes you dash.**
- **The escort cards and the BRING THEM HERE shelter marker show up in Invasion.** They'd never appeared in a live round, because we were looking up the Front by the wrong name.
- **The DRIVE THE COMMAND TRUCK card no longer breaks on tablets and PC.**
- **Phone players can change class on the rematch screen again.** It had them locked in as a Citizen.
- **Dr. Peepers on touch heals the teammate under his crosshair,** not whoever was standing behind the button he tapped.
- **Bowlers aren't teleported off their own approach after every throw.**
- **Your own Bowling celebration shows up even if your display name isn't your username.**
- **The Bowling Results card shows up even if saving your XP fails,** and it tells you so.
- **No more MATCH VICTORY when you're the only one bowling.**
