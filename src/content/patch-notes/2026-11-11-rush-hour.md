## 🆕 New
- **Pants Rush is open to everyone.** Pick Invasion on the PLAY menu, then Pants Rush. This time you're the alien: steal pants as the Commander or the Overlord, alone or with a crew of up to four. It turns out the suburbs have a lot of pants.
- **Six phases, six bosses.** Raid the High School, Garage Alley, Playground Plaza, Gas & Go and the Water Tower. Fill each phase's pants quota before the clock runs out to bring out its boss: The Hall Monitor, The Seam Ripper, The Recess Menace, Dr. Peepers and Needle Eye.
- **The Pants Factory finale.** Break into the factory, stop the cannons, smash the glass and carry the Golden Pants back to the mothership's beam. Then you turn giant and get to wreck the place for a while. We think that's a fair reward for a day's work.
- **Bosses don't just fall over.** Knock a boss down, then depants it to finish it. On Normal and Hard they get a second health bar and their own tricks, and the big finishes go into slow motion.
- **Play as a minion.** In co-op, two players are the Commander or the Overlord and up to two more play as their minions, grabbing citizens and carrying them to the beam. Co-op crews also get extra waves to deal with.
- **Revive your crew.** Stand next to a downed teammate and hold the button to get them back up. Minions can do it too.
- **Time Trial or Unlimited, on Easy, Normal or Hard.** Time Trial scores your run and gives out medals, and the best scores and fastest times go on the rankings, split by difficulty and by solo or co-op. Unlimited takes the clock away.
- **Pick your starting phase.** Phases you've cleared stay unlocked, so you can start a run from any of them, and each one shows your best time.
- **Pants Rush pays.** Clearing a phase earns XP, coins and class mastery, with a bonus the first time you clear it on each difficulty. Your level, cosmetics, emotes and settings come with you.
- **A guided first phase.** New players get a short tutorial in the High School that covers aiming, depantsing and the Pants Storm, which marks the edge of the phase you're in.
- **Play Solo, Find Co-op or Play with Friends.** Solo gets you a private run, and Find Co-op matches you with other players looking for a crew.
- **Settings work in Pants Rush:** Leave Match, Change Character, and Restart Phase if you're the host.
- **The Overlord's Mind Shield works by itself.** It recharges after it breaks, and he brings two Iron Mauler bodyguards.
- **Mini-bosses show through walls,** so you can find the one hiding upstairs.
- **Banking pants heals you,** and the mothership hovers over every drop-off pad so you always know where to go.
- **Pants Rush menus have character art** and are laid out for phones, tablets and controllers.
- **Survivors mini-bosses drop a Lint Cache chest.** The first player to reach it opens it for the whole squad. Everyone gets their own loot: upgrades for cards they already hold, Lint and a bit of scrap.
- **Lucky Sock in the Lint Shop.** Buy luck with Lint before the run starts: rarer cards show up more often and boss chests give more.
- **Cul-de-sac opens with a crowd.** A ring of Minions is already waiting when wave 1 starts.

## 🔧 Changes
- **The Lobby's menus got a makeover.** Every screen has the same look now, with cream panels, denim and comic letters. Phones get proper sideways and upright layouts, buttons react when you press them, and a button that can't be pressed tells you why.
- **Hall of Pants in the Lobby leads with the leaderboards,** and the Shop's filters now sit behind one "Show:" picker.
- **The AFK Lounge launches with a 3-2-1 countdown and a Cancel button,** like every other game.
- **Survivors has a new start menu.** Pick your class, then pick a stage, then play. Each stage shows what's different about it, what it can pay and how to unlock it.
- **Survivors menus match the new look,** and Play, Class Paths and Shop stay hidden during a run so you can't open them by accident.
- **The RETURN TO LOBBY sign in the middle of Cul-de-sac is gone.** Back to Lobby is in the menu now. It was a working exit in the middle of a fight. Bold of us.
- **Survivors feels juicier.** Scrap pickups tick, the level bar glides and flashes, level-up cards ripple in (rarer ones flare), Evolutions get an EVOLVED banner, and damage numbers land bigger and don't stack on top of each other.
- **The Survivors Results card sums up your run in one line.** It also shows NEW BEST for kills and damage and tells you when you can afford something in the Lint Shop. PLAY AGAIN takes you straight to picking a stage.
- **Riot Stance braces whenever you stand still, even while shooting.** Before, auto-shoot kept it switched off almost all the time.
- **Warm-Cycle Barrel isn't offered to Gatling Hamper holders any more.** It did almost nothing for them.
- **Bowling fits on one screen.** Pick who's playing (Just Me, Vs Computer or With Friends), pick Mayhem or Classic, and hit Bowl!. Leave Lane, Change Lane and Look Around are right there, and Classic is easy to find again.
- **Bowling has one results card,** with your score, your best and a big Play Again. The Pro Shop is a tab in the Shop now, and the Arcade is called "Play While You Wait".
- **Bowling alone or against the computer starts right away,** and there's no turn clock when you're the only person on the lane.
- **The Tractor Ball pulls harder toward the head pin,** with a wider, glowier beam.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **The Immovable Gusset really adds the max health its card says.** It was adding much less, and with a maxed-out shop it could even take some away. Not great for a card called "Immovable".
- **The Tailor Engineer has the same health in Survivors as in Invasion.**
- **A Survivors run still pays if the server restarts,** or if the last player drops and doesn't come back. You get paid as if you'd left. Before, you got nothing.
- **A Survivors teammate who drops and rejoins mid-wave no longer weakens the boss for good.**
- **Bowling Pro Shop balls have their colors back,** and the Turkey ball is back in the shop.
- **Strike streaks of five or more get the right number.** Every one of them used to be "FOUR". Our announcer can count now.
- **The Bowling lane picker shows who's really on each lane** when you arrive.
- **Bowlers no longer get nudged off the approach during the computer's turn.**
- **The Lobby welcome screen is full size on phones again,** and your first tap on it always counts.
- **In Invasion, the camera-switch confirmation opens on top of Settings again.**
- **Lobby Settings:** a Go To shortcut asks whether to apply your unsaved changes first, and How to Play opens properly. On a controller, the Shop's purchase confirmation starts on Keep browsing.
