## 🆕 New
- **A front door for the Lobby.** A new welcome screen puts the games one tap away: Bowling, Survivors, Invasion, the Arcade and the AFK Lounge, with Bowling marked as a great first game. On a phone, a big PLAY button opens the PLAY menu. It replaces the long beta notice, which we're told nobody read.
- **Sky Sprint.** A Bumper Saucer race high above the Lobby. Fly your saucer through the portal, thread 18 rings past moving obstacles, race other pilots or go solo, and finish to fireworks. A floating scoreboard keeps the best times, your records go in the Hall of Pants, and the saucer boost lasts longer for everyone.
- **The shooting range is a band now.** Shoot the keys on the range walls to play notes, and nearby gunfire quiets down so you can hear yourself. Only people in the range can hear you, which is probably for the best.
- **Perfect Runs.** Finish the Gauntlet or the Spin Cycle without dying or going back to a checkpoint and it counts as a Perfect Run. Your total shows in My Records, and the top three are on the obby's Hall of Pants plaque.
- **Survivors supply drops.** In the early waves, a supply crate crashes into the grass. Stand in its ring to unpack it while the minions pile in. Open it in time and everyone gets a free card pick; take too long and the minions loot it and send in Elites. Sadly, no pants inside.
- **Survivors characters talk now.** The classes bring their Invasion voice lines, plus new ones for getting knocked down, being revived, big kills and the end of a run. The Bargain Commander has plenty to say too, and a squadmate gets the last word. Every line has captions.
- **Party bonus.** Bowling and Survivors give a little extra XP for each extra player in the game, up to a cap, and the portal signs have the details. In Survivors it goes to class mastery. Friends: now with a measurable benefit.
- **Bowling: one tap to bowl.** Arriving at Bowling puts you straight on the best open lane, and BOWL NOW (Enter, or (Y) on a controller) does the same. A solo game skips the countdown, and the intro is shorter.
- **Bowling: a throw coach.** Big arrows point at AIM, POWER and CURVE on every throw, plus PINS LEFT on your second ball and SPECIAL BALLS in Mayhem. Once you've got it, hold NEVER SHOW AGAIN (H, R3 on a controller, or hold the button itself), or turn off Bowling tips in Settings. Your very first throw rolls straight, and nobody calls you a slowpoke in your first game.
- **Bowling: a closer look.** Hold right-click, or LT on a controller, while you aim to move in behind the ball and see the lane arrows.
- **Invasion: Start New Match or Join Existing Match.** The PLAY menu can now start a fresh Invasion match, still open to anyone who wants to join, or list the games already running. Start New keeps the class you picked in Mission Control.

## 🔧 Changes
- **Small Invasion teams get backup.** When only a few humans are defending, the Collector bots ease off even if human Collectors are playing, Defender bots aim better and react faster, and Dr. Peepers goes looking for the humans who actually need healing. Human Defenders also hit a little harder, have more health and stamina, and reload and respawn a bit faster in Final Stand.
- **Collector bots carrying Golden Pants can't Rocket Jump or Dash any more.** Stolen pants, no shortcuts.
- **The Invasion launch countdown is shorter,** since you can join a match after it starts anyway.
- **Camera settings make more sense.** If you never picked a camera, you get Roblox's Default camera everywhere except Invasion, which uses the NOPAS camera. Everyone starts their next Invasion in the NOPAS camera, and whatever you choose after that sticks. Switching to Default in Invasion asks first, because aiming is much harder without first-person, and the camera tip no longer pops up in the Lobby.
- **Bowling and Survivors games start open,** so other players can drop in and team up. Want it to yourself? The host can switch the game to Solo or Friends once you're in.
- **Bowling plays a little more like bowling.** Hitting the head pin dead-on with a straight first ball leaves more splits, and the aim number is gone, so aim with the arrows on the lane.
- **The Bowling curve bar sweeps smoothly and has a STRAIGHT zone in the middle,** so straight balls are easier to land and small curves still work.
- **Controller players pick Mayhem balls from a menu that pauses the throw.** Browse with the D-pad, press (A) to equip and (B) to back out. (B) also steps your throw back from Curve to Power to Aim.
- **Survivors on phones got a rebuild.** A compact status HUD in the corner, the weapon bar lower down, a sprint button, class select, upgrade and revive screens that fit, a one-line Engineer build count, and Place here, Build and Cancel buttons for building. Notices move out of the way, too.
- **Phones and tablets get a hand in Survivors:** an extra revive each run, auto-shoot that also catches enemies right next to you, a longer scrap pickup reach and slightly faster health regen.
- **New Survivors players start with auto-shoot on,** and their first two training runs each come with a free revive. Saved settings stay as they were.
- **Shooting range targets move around now,** and run off in a panic when you knock their pants off. Anyone can grab the pants they drop and send them up to the mothership, the beams work faster, and the bullseyes stand up straight.
- **Falling off the Lobby is a lot less dramatic.** You're put back where you last stood, with no damage, and the plaza has guardrails. The Gauntlet still sends you to your checkpoint, because rules are rules.
- **Bumper Saucer bumps land more easily,** and the saucers stay inside their airspace.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **The Bowling camera follows your aim and your ball again.**
- **You arrive in Invasion already dressed for your class,** instead of changing outfits when Setup starts.
- **Characters no longer spawn into Invasion extra tall.** We were measuring them while they were lying down.
- **Defenders no longer see a flickering Board Ship prompt** at the mothership beam.
- **Portal pads stop getting stuck on LAUNCHING...** If you step on a Bowling or Survivors pad just after another group left, you get your own game straight away.
- **The Lobby works better on phones.** The PLAY menu fits on a landscape screen, the range and saucer HUDs shrink, the shop, Class Paths, records, emote and photo screens stay on screen, and dragging the right side of the screen turns the camera anywhere, not just at the shooting range.
- **An invisible wall no longer blocks the way to the Spin Cycle.**
- **The directory board by the fountain lists the Gauntlet Obby.** It was hiding.
- **Shooting range targets no longer get stuck on the ceiling.**
- **Minion Invaders enemies fly back to their formation after a dive** instead of chasing it off the screen.
- **The Bargain Commander's depants pulse works now.** Get hit and you're in the blocky safety shorts until the wave ends.
- **The Survivors revive tip says to stand close to your teammate,** not to hold a button that never existed. Sorry about that.
- **The Survivors Powers menu opens on top of everything,** and you can't pick a card by accident behind it.
- **Survivors Engineers can place dispensers near the spawn.**
- **The Bowling welcome guide has a close button,** and (B) closes it on a controller.
- **The Pro Shop's "XP to next level" shows what you have left,** not the whole level's cost.
