## 🆕 New
- **The AFK Lounge.** A new place where you can sit on a couch and slowly earn coins while you're away, up to a daily limit. Find it on the PLAY menu, and BACK TO LOBBY takes you home. Finally, a game mode we're all good at.
- **Pause your solo Survivors runs.** Playing alone? Press P, use the new PAUSE option in Settings, or hold the View/Menu button on a controller. Everything freezes until you're back. Each run has a limited pause bank that clearing a boss wave tops up, and someone joining ends the pause.
- **A controller pointer in every menu.** Menus in the lobby, Survivors and Invasion now open with a pointer: the left stick moves it, (A) clicks, the right stick scrolls, and the D-pad switches back to button-by-button. Settings sliders work with a controller too.
- **Inspect cosmetics up close.** Click the Quartermaster preview to open a big inspection window, and browse a collection that finally gets most of the screen.
- **Solo or Open.** Launching Bowling or Survivors from the PLAY menu now asks whether you want a solo game or an open one, then counts down so you can change your mind.
- **Bowling: JOIN A LANE.** A new menu lists every lane and lets you jump straight in, and glowing JOIN LANE HERE! labels mark the open ones.
- **Bowling: LEAVE and END GAME.** You can now leave in the middle of a game, and the host can end it for everyone. Both ask first, so nobody rage-quits by accident.

## 🔧 Changes
- **Alien bots go easier on small groups.** When only a few humans are playing and none of them is an alien, the bot Collectors aim and react a little slower.
- **Mission Control explains that you don't need a full team.** You can learn against bots and still earn XP.
- **Bowling's aim preview shows your real ball:** the same skin, size and color you're about to throw.
- **Bowling on phones** got a cleaner layout, and the arcade games on touch screens now play from anywhere on the screen.
- **Survivors extraction meteors are spread out,** and they now look and sound like proper explosions.
- **Survivors cards show what they apply to,** your Lint balance is easier to find, and the Static Cling Storm now explains itself on screen.
- **Overstarched Payload** now only shows up for classes it actually helps, and its Tailor Engineer description finally matches what it does.
- **Bots chase drones harder** in Invasion.
- **Finishing a daily or weekly challenge** now pops a celebration you can actually see.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **Dropped GoldenPants no longer sink into the factory floor,** so they can be picked up again.
- **An alien who secures the GoldenPants now always becomes the Giant,** even if they're hit at the last second.
- **Bots and Citizens stop crowding the jail pens during Setup.** There was nothing to do there yet. They just really liked the view.
- **Solo Play Again works in Invasion** and keeps the class you just picked.
- **Tailor Engineer turrets now hurt the Survivors Overlord boss.**
- **Blart Bash and dashes can't push anyone through walls.**
- **The Great Smuggler can't escape the arena,** and an escape no longer counts as a kill.
- **Bowling bench seats stay put** when your turn starts, instead of riding into the lane with you.
- **A Bowling lane reset no longer loses your progress** from that game.
- **Bowling's Minion Man arcade** no longer ends instantly after giving up on Hard, and touch arcade buttons moved away from Jump.
- **The Spin Cycle portal no longer blocks the Bumper Saucers walkway,** and challenge rewards no longer replay when you rejoin.
