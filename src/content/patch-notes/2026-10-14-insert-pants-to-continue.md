## 🆕 New
- **The lobby has an arcade.** The Wardrobe is now Arcade & Gear, with four cabinets: Minion Invaders, Minion Man, Minion Plumber and Minion Kong. A board on the wall shows the top scores, and the cosmetics are still there, so you can lose at Kong and buy a hat about it.
- **Invasion matches hold up to 18 players.** More room for Defenders and Citizens, more spawn points in Final Stand, and a results board that fits everyone. If the Defender slots are full, you'll be asked to play a Citizen instead.
- **Join a match already in progress.** You can hop into Invasion until about halfway through, and a quick class picker works with mouse, touch and controller. Arriving late no longer gets you kicked when Setup ends.
- **Phones can play Defenders.** Phone players can now pick any Defender class in Invasion, with a compact HUD, rebuilt touch controls, a ZOOM button and a little aim help. Collectors are still for tablets, PCs and consoles.
- **Bring a Buddy.** Press INVITE FRIENDS in the lobby. When a friend who's new to the game, or back after a long break, finishes a match with you, you both earn the Pants Buddies title and some coins. Finishing a match with Roblox friends also earns bonus XP.
- **A narrated Invasion tutorial.** A short broadcast with voice, captions and animated characters explains how Invasion works. Watch it at the new kiosk outside Mission Control, or open help from the menu. It features a Pants Factory, labeled in crayon for your convenience.
- **Bowling pins have opinions now.** A lone survivor gloats, split pins say hi to each other, gutter balls get a stadium wave, strikes set off a pants geyser, and the head pin collects bumps and bandages as the game goes on. Pins may also throw a rubber chicken at anyone in the pit. None of it changes your score. All of it is personal.
- **Separate Bowling high scores for Classic and Mayhem,** so special balls no longer compete with plain bowling. Your old best stays on show as your Legacy Best, and the new boards start fresh.
- **Minion Plumber: Quick Connect.** A new mode that clears as soon as every color pair is connected, with no need to fill every square. Classic is still there, and each mode has its own high scores.
- **Free the mouse in Bowling.** Tap Left Alt to get a pointer and click any button on screen, then tap again to go back to mouse-look. You can change the key in Settings.

## 🔧 Changes
- **The arcade games got a glow-up.** All four have new art, effects and HUDs. Minion Invaders adds boss ships between waves, and your weapon upgrades now last until you lose a life.
- **Arcade games on phones and tablets** now go fullscreen, steer more smoothly with your thumb, and keep the camera still behind the game. Minion Plumber paints exactly where your finger or pointer goes, and you can't cut a pipe by crossing it.
- **Setup is shorter, and the minions don't wait for Invasion any more.** They now arrive in waves during Setup, and a player Overlord picks where they land.
- **Collectors earn XP for jailing Citizens with the drone during Setup.**
- **The Invasion fight moves around less.** Each match now has two longer battles instead of three shorter ones, each with one big event.
- **The Charged Citizen event gives Defenders a short countdown** to get there before the Collectors can strike.
- **Survivors cards got a big cleanup.** You're no longer offered cards that do nothing for your class (the Tailor Engineer was getting a lot of these), evolutions you can't build say NOT AVAILABLE THIS RUN, a long list of cards got stronger or easier to see working, and a few got stricter rules.
- **Survivors main-gun cards now power up only your main gun,** the one in slot 1.
- **Late joiners in Survivors start at a catch-up level** instead of out-leveling everyone who got there first.
- **Knocked-down Bowling pins stay where they fell** until the beam collects them, and they ride up dizzy, with stars and a slide whistle.
- **Settings got a makeover:** illustrated icons, layouts that fit phones and tablets, button prompts that match your device, and a big Play button.
- **Bowling on phones:** the ARCADE button no longer sits on top of the Pro Shop, and the lane lobby fits on the screen.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **Survivors auto-shoot now hits enemies right in front of you.** It used to ignore anyone standing too close, and the Tailor Engineer's auto-thrown Spanner sailed straight over their heads.
- **Survivors power-up cards no longer sit on top of Settings,** so clicking a setting can't pick a card by accident.
- **About 30 Survivors card descriptions now match what the cards actually do.** We read them all. Some of them twice.
- **Controller players get a selection in the Bowling lane menu again,** and the Mayhem ball chips no longer steal (A) from your throw.
- **Boss Bowl brings back players who spawn late or wander out of the arena,** and clicking while your aim crosses the scoreboard no longer opens the score card.
- **The Lobby Event Log is back for controller players.**
- **Minion Plumber on touch screens connects the last pipe** instead of stopping one square short.
- **Falling into the Bowling pit makes a sound now.** It used to be silent. Now it thuds.
